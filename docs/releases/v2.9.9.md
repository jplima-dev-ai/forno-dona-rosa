# v2.9.9 — Mobile Design Refinement

## Summary

The 2.9.x line refines the existing Real Commerce experience for narrow touch screens without adding a new customer workflow. The goal is faster scanning, clearer action hierarchy and lower vertical cost while preserving semantic order, keyboard operation and the white-label architecture.

## Highlights

- Shorter mobile header with logo, Bag and menu retained.
- Cleaner mobile Hero with one-column CTAs and edge-to-edge product imagery.
- Compact three-part artisan process summary.
- Horizontal fast-scan product cards on phones.
- Horizontal category filter rail and denser search surface.
- Full-height mobile Bag with larger quantity targets and stronger subtotal hierarchy.
- Refined full-height checkout with compact choices and safe-area handling.
- Circular secondary Rosa launcher on phones to reduce competition with commerce actions.
- Small-phone, landscape, tablet and large-screen adaptation rules based on content needs.
- Forced-colors and reduced-motion fallbacks preserved.

## Accessibility and responsive contract

The release does not duplicate desktop and mobile content. DOM order remains the source for reading and focus order; CSS changes presentation only. Touch targets remain at least approximately 3rem in the refined surfaces, and fixed controls account for safe-area insets.

Automated/static checks do not replace real assistive-technology or real-device validation. NVDA, JAWS, Narrator, TalkBack, VoiceOver, Playwright/Axe and physical-device testing must be reported separately when actually executed.

## Upgrade

No data migration is required from v2.8.9. Runtime version and service-worker cache are bumped to v2.9.9.
