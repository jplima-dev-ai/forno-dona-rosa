# v2.8.9 — Real Commerce Operations

## Purpose

Turn the fast-purchase 2.7 experience into a realistic daily ordering flow without adding a backend or forcing the customer through unnecessary fields.

## Customer flow

Cardápio → Sacola → Delivery or pickup → ASAP or schedule → Pix or cash → Review → WhatsApp.

## Operational rules

- Delivery: Serra — ES, with CEP lookup and manual fallback.
- Pickup: enabled at the configured pizzeria address.
- Payment: Pix or cash. Cash can include an optional change amount.
- Scheduling: enabled within configured business hours, with a minimum lead time and a seven-day horizon.
- Delivery fee and ETA: configurable; the Dona Rosa reference currently confirms them on WhatsApp rather than presenting invented numbers.
- Availability: configurable product IDs can be marked unavailable and cannot be added to the Sacola.
- Analytics: disabled by default.

## Reliability changes

Persisted Sacola items are rebuilt from the current catalog. Unavailable products are removed and stale prices are recalculated. Multi-tab changes synchronize through the `storage` event. Offline and 404 recovery pages provide a clear route back to the menu.

## Accessibility evidence boundary

The release preserves native controls, fieldset/legend grouping, explicit labels, error associations, focus-first review and non-automatic WhatsApp disclosure. This artifact does not claim NVDA, JAWS, Narrator, TalkBack or VoiceOver testing unless those tests are subsequently executed on a real target environment.
